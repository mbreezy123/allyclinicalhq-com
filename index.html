The problem

Small gaps that cost real time and money

01

Sessions start by reconstructing the week

The first minutes often go to remembering what happened since last time.

02

Ideas from session fade by Wednesday

Without a small prompt, the one thing a client meant to try is easy to lose.

03

Most tools are too heavy

Many between-session apps ask clients for more than they'll do. This is one page and about two minutes.

FAQ

Straight answers

Who are you?

I'm Matt Breen, the founder. Ally Clinical is a small, new, independent service that I run myself, from New York. You deal with me directly.

What does it cost?

The template is free. If a paid version ever exists, it will be simple flat pricing quoted upfront before you commit.

Is this a clinical tool?

No. It's a general organizing template, not a clinical instrument or assessment, and it doesn't replace your clinical judgment or records. If a client is in crisis or feels unsafe, they shouldn't wait for the next session: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911.

What do you need from me?

Nothing about your clients. Just an email address to send the template to, and, if you're willing, your honest feedback after trying it.

Do you contact my clients?

No, never. I don't receive client information or PHI, and I have no way to reach your clients. The template stays in your hands.

How do I opt out?

Reply "no" to any email from me and I won't email you again. You can also ask me to delete anything you've sent by writing to matt@allyclinicalhq.com, and I will.
